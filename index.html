Overview

A recycled phone case turned into a sound box: each of the 10 keypad keys (1 to 9 and 0) is a rubber push button that plays its own short audio clip through a small speaker.

How it works

An ESP32 dev board reads the 10 buttons (active-low, internal pull-ups) and plays WAV files stored in its flash (LittleFS). The audio goes out over I2S to a MAX98357A amplifier that drives an 8 Ω speaker directly.

Trigger keys
10
WAV sample rate
16 kHz

Mono, 16-bit PCM recommended. About 87 s of audio fit in total at that format.

Playback rules

  • At power-up a short 3-note chime plays.
  • Press a key while idle: its clip plays to the end.
  • Press any key while a clip plays: playback stops (no new clip starts).
  • The LED blinks while audio plays and is off when idle.

Power

Always on from a 5 V USB adapter through the ESP32 board's USB port; there is no power switch. The amplifier takes 5 V from the board's VIN pin.

Firmware

The PlatformIO project lives in the firmware/ folder of the repository, with build, flashing and audio replacement instructions in firmware/README.md.
